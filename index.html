Tim Hortons Bagel Menu with Prices (2026) — What to Order and What It'll Cost You

If you're looking at the Tim Hortons bagel menu for the first time, you probably want to know two things — what bagels they actually have, and how much you're going to spend. That's exactly what this guide covers.

What You're Working With at Tim Hortons

Tim Hortons doesn't overcomplicate their bagel menu, and that's a good thing. You're choosing from a handful of well-made, freshly baked bagels in both classic and specialty flavors. Each one is baked daily at the location, so you're always getting something warm and fresh — not something that's been sitting in a bag since yesterday.

The price range is reasonable. A single bagel will run you somewhere between $1.69 and $2.19 USD depending on the variety. If you're in Canada, you're looking at roughly $1.39 to $1.89 CAD. Add a spread like cream cheese and you'll pay a little more, but you're still looking at a complete breakfast for under $3 most of the time.

Each Bagel Explained — So You Know What You're Actually Getting

Plain Bagel — $1.69 USD

This is the most basic option and the cheapest one on the menu. If you want something simple that you can load up with your own topping, start here. It's soft, mild, and works with anything — butter, jam, cream cheese, peanut butter. It's also vegan, which matters if you're avoiding dairy or eggs.

Sesame Seed Bagel — $1.69 USD

Same price as the plain but with a light nutty crunch from toasted sesame seeds on the outside. If you want just a little more flavor without going into specialty territory, this is a good call. It's also vegan-friendly and one of the original bagels Tim Hortons has offered since the beginning.

Everything Bagel — $1.89 USD

This is the one most people reach for, and for good reason. You get sesame seeds, poppy seeds, dried onion, garlic, and salt all baked onto the outside. It hits savory, slightly salty, and aromatic all at once. At 290 calories and under $2, it gives you the most flavor per dollar on this menu. If you don't know what to get, start here.

Cinnamon Raisin Bagel — $1.89 USD

If you prefer something on the sweeter side in the morning, this is your pick. It has juicy raisins baked through the dough with a warm cinnamon flavor throughout. Toast it and put a little cream cheese or butter on it — that's where it really works. It comes in at 304 calories, which is slightly more than the savory options but nothing drastic.

12-Grain Bagel — $1.99 USD

This one is for you if you want something that feels a bit more substantial and wholesome. It has more fiber and a heartier bite compared to the standard bagels. The price is a little higher at $1.99, but the nutritional trade-off makes sense if you're trying to eat something with a bit more substance. It's also vegan, so plant-based eaters can go for this one without any concern.

Jalapeno Asiago Bagel — $1.99 USD

This is where things get interesting. You get heat from the jalapeños and a creamy, slightly sharp flavor from the Asiago cheese, all baked into the dough. It's not overwhelmingly spicy — it's more of a kick than a burn. If you like bold flavors with your morning coffee, this one will hold your attention. At $1.99 it's priced the same as the 12-Grain, and it delivers a lot more personality.

4 Cheese Bagel — $2.19 USD

The most expensive single bagel on the menu and the most indulgent. Four types of cheese are blended into and onto the bagel, giving it a rich, savory depth that doesn't need much topping. You can honestly eat this one plain and it's still satisfying. If you want something that feels closer to a real meal without ordering a sandwich, this is the one.

What You Can Add to Your Bagel

You don't have to eat your bagel plain. Here's what Tim Hortons lets you add:

  • Plain Cream Cheese — The most popular spread. Adds about $0.60–$0.80 to your total.
  • Herb & Garlic Cream Cheese — Works especially well with the Everything or Jalapeno Asiago bagel.
  • Butter — Simple and good. Best on the Cinnamon Raisin or Plain.
  • Peanut Butter or Jam — Available at select locations, and a good vegan-friendly option.

One thing you should always do — ask for it toasted. There's no extra charge, and it makes a noticeable difference in texture and flavor. A toasted bagel with cream cheese is a completely different experience than a cold, untoasted one.

Combos and Bundles — Where You Save Money

Bagel + Coffee Combo Pair any bagel with a brewed coffee and you're looking at roughly $3.49–$4.49 USD for the whole thing. That's a solid, filling breakfast for under $5. If you're a regular, this is the default move.

Bagel with Cream Cheese + Coffee Step it up slightly and you're still well under $5 in most locations — usually landing around $4.00–$5.00 USD. A real breakfast, not just a snack.

6 Assorted Bagels Pack — $7.99–$9.99 USD If you're buying for more than yourself — family, office, a group — this is the smart order. You typically get a mix of Everything, Plain, Cinnamon Raisin, and 12-Grain. That works out to roughly $1.33–$1.66 per bagel, which is cheaper than buying them individually. Check the Tim Hortons app for deal prices before you order, because combos and bundle promotions rotate.

Which Bagels Are Vegan?

If you're avoiding animal products, you need to know this before you order:

  • Plain Bagel — Vegan
  • Sesame Seed Bagel — Vegan
  • 12-Grain Bagel — Vegan
  • Everything Bagel — Contains honey
  • Cinnamon Raisin — Contains dairy
  • Jalapeno Asiago & 4 Cheese — Contain dairy and/or eggs

Keep in mind that Tim Hortons bakeries are shared environments, so cross-contact is possible. If you have a serious allergy, check with the specific store directly.

FAQs - Tim Hortons Bagel

  1. How much is a bagel at Tim Hortons?
    A: Somewhere between $1.69 and $2.19 USD for a single bagel, depending on which variety you order. The plain and sesame seed are the cheapest at $1.69. The 4 Cheese is the most expensive at $2.19.
  2. Does Tim Hortons toast their bagels?
    Yes, and you should always ask for it toasted. There's no extra charge and it makes a real difference.
  3. What's the best bagel at Tim Hortons?
    The Everything Bagel is the most popular for a reason. If you're new to the menu, that's where you should start.
  4. Are Tim Hortons bagels made fresh?
    Yes. Each location bakes them fresh every day, so you're not getting something pre-packaged.
  5. Can I order bagels through the app?
    Yes. The Tim Hortons app lets you order ahead for pickup or delivery, and it often has exclusive deals that aren't available in-store.
  6. Does Tim Hortons have gluten-free bagels?
    Not as of 2026. All bagels on the menu contain wheat flour.
